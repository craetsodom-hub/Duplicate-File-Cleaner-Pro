Duplicate File Cleaner Pro Privacy Policy

Duplicate File Cleaner Pro processes files and paths from folders that you explicitly select on your device.

Local processing

File contents, filenames, paths, scan results, and cleanup results are processed locally. The app does not upload them.

No accounts or analytics

The app has no account system, telemetry, analytics, cloud processing, scan history, cleanup history, or database.

Cleanup

If you confirm cleanup, approved candidates are moved through the Windows Recycle Bin. The app does not permanently delete files.

Stored preference

The only persisted preference is your local appearance choice: System, Light, or Dark.

Contact

A support contact will be supplied by the Store publisher when this policy is hosted publicly.
